### Why it does not curl, diagnosed

The planter builds at 2.0500 σ against a relaxed 1.7651 σ (**D9**), so the term reads the starting
membrane as under-crowded and inflates *every* head at step 0. That **global** inflation is first-order
and lowers `P` everywhere; the **leaflet asymmetry** that makes curvature is second-order and is
swamped. The term changes the phase before it can bend the phase it started in.

## 3. Retracted this session

**Both previous MLP nulls (B6 affinity, B7 shape).** `n_ref` was registered as "the close-packed 2-D
coordination number (geometry), not a fit". Two errors compound: `coord_weight` returns a smooth
**kernel weight**, not a count (0.107 at the 2.05 σ head spacing, so two neighbours score 0.21, not 2);
and a 2-D leaflet is a **line**, so a head has two in-leaflet neighbours, not six. The descriptor was
also narrowed to head-only on 2026-09-09 and `n_ref` was never re-derived.

Measured through `Field.coordination` — the same code the force reads — head coordination is 0.2070
against `n_ref = 6.0`. The bracket was therefore **1.2414 for every head in every state, spread 0.4 %**:
a uniform 24 % inflation, not a modulation. The outer−inner wedge on a ring, which *is* the mechanism,
was **+0.10 %**; at a derived `n_ref`, **+3.77 %**.

What B7 did test is kept under its own name: *a uniform 24 % head enlargement does not curl a ribbon.*

**Consequence for the standing story:** the flat-ribbon protocol is back to **five** nulls, all
symmetric pair terms — so the structural explanation ("a pair potential cannot express a difference
between leaflets") covers every surviving null again. The 2026-09-09 claim that it no longer covered
the data is withdrawn.

**Also withdrawn, before it was used:** my own supplied-vs-needed curvature prediction. Both halves
failed their controls — the leaflet split is invalid (only the N=224 planted ring separates; on real
vesicles it returns d ≈ 9–10 σ for 4 σ lipids), and my claim that the bounded response caps the wedge
was simply wrong (the wedge rises monotonically, 0.105 → 3.95 across the ladder). Nothing reached a
committed file.
