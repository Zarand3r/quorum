objective & physical law — living = inference = optimization
No separate train / infer / run phases: each tick IS one transformer forward pass, so running the sim is the computation is the optimization — no separation. The physical law is baked into the grounded attention: the dock score ⟨Cᵢ, M·Cⱼ⟩ is a real binding energy (by Parseval, attention = contour overlap). The dynamics descend a packing/binding free energy Φ (↓ steric clash ⟨Cᵢ,Cⱼ⟩, ↑ complementary fit ⟨Cᵢ,M·Cⱼ⟩) driven off-equilibrium by a non-gradient skew flux zJ so it never settles — ẋ = −D∇Φ + J, the potential-flux form of a living, dissipative system. So there IS a thermodynamic objective (Φ); the inference relaxing it is the living.

Honest caveat: in this engine the network weights are fixed — what is optimized is the state (the embeddings / the fold), not the parameters. The full “weights that learn while alive” (training = living, one clock) is the harder predictive-plasticity direction we tried and set aside. Aliveness above is measured, never fed back (measure-don't-reward).
